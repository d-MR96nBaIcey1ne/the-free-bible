# The Free Quarry

**Working documents. Not finished works.**

Everything in this directory is mid-build — scaffolds, blooms awaiting a chisel, drafts in transit between hands. Finished stones live elsewhere in this repository. If you have arrived here looking for the corpus, you want the numbered files at the root and the `Prayers-for-Life` directory.

---

## Why it is public

The Free Bible is built under a two-hand discipline. One hand quarries the bloom from the source; a second, different hand scaffolds it cold beforehand and chisels it once afterward. A hand cannot audit its own reach.

That method produces working documents, and this directory is where they pass between hands.

Keeping it open is deliberate. The finished stones already carry their chisel notes, their declared textual forks, and their refusals printed beside their cuts — what was reached for and put back, alongside what was kept. Hiding the workspace while publishing the workings would be the odd choice, not this.

**Expect errors here.** A document in this directory has not been verified against a second witness, has not been chiselled, and may contain a reading that the finished stone later cuts. That is what a quarry is.

---

## What you may find

- **Scaffolds** — structure and briefs, with the source text verified and the renderings left empty.
- **Bloomed drafts** — a rendering made, awaiting the second hand.
- **Chiselled returns** — a draft with cuts made and refusals recorded, on its way back.

Files are removed once the work lands on the shelf. Nothing here is canonical, and nothing here should be quoted as the corpus.
